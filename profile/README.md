# OLGAX-com 🚀

**O**pen **L**aunchpad for **G**rowth **A**nd e**X**ecution

Building open-source software, developer tools, and business solutions for small businesses, developers, freelancers, and indie makers.

OLGAX is an open-source software initiative building practical, self-hostable products with a community-driven development approach. Our mission is to make useful software accessible while creating opportunities for developers to learn, collaborate, and contribute.

## 🚀 Our Products

- **[OLGAX POS](https://github.com/OLGAX-com/olgax-pos)** – An open-source, offline-capable Point of Sale system for modern businesses. Free forever for self-hosted deployments.
- **[OLGAX DXP](https://github.com/OLGAX-com/olgax-dxp)** – An open-source, self-hostable Digital Experience Platform combining content management and visual page building.
- **[OLGAX Developer Portal](https://developers.olgax.com/)** – Our developer community platform for profiles, documentation, mentorship, contributor programs, and collaboration.
- **OLGAX Cloud** – Coming soon. A cloud platform designed to simplify application deployment, hosting, and infrastructure management.

## 🛠️ What We Focus On

- Open-source and self-hostable software
- Practical solutions for real-world problems
- Developer-friendly tools and experiences
- Privacy, flexibility, and reduced vendor lock-in
- Community-driven development and collaboration
